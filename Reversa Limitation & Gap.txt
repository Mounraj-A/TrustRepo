The one-sentence research gap

Reversa transforms legacy repositories into operational specifications and assigns confidence to generated claims, but the confidence is produced by the same generation pipeline, is not independently audited, is not calibrated against ground truth, and is not validated using multiple independent evidence sources such as code, tests, commits, issues, and pull requests.
